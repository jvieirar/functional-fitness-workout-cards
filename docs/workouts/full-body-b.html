THURSDAY · OR FRIDAY AFTER WED BADMINTON

Full-body B

Use Thursday when recovered. If Tuesday and Wednesday both contain badminton, make Thursday mobility and easy skill work, move Full-body B to Friday, and omit Full-body C.

36–42 MINTHURSDAY JUMPS ONLY WHEN ELIGIBLE · NEVER BEFORE SAME-DAY BADMINTON

Complete instructions

Use Thursday when recovered. If Tuesday and Wednesday both contain badminton, make Thursday mobility and easy skill work, move Full-body B to Friday, and omit Full-body C.

Preparation, pistol practice, and eligible plyometrics

  • Daily mobility shortened to 5 minutes.
  • Pistol-squat practice — 7 minutes.
  • Thursday only, choose one: lateral bound and stick 2 × 4 per side; pogo hops 2 × 8–10; or on the following eligible session, split-step to random left/right cue 3 × 10 seconds.
  • Omit after Wednesday badminton and whenever badminton occurs later that day.
  • Do not perform Full-body B lower-body strength immediately before same-day badminton.

Strength superset 1 — 3 rounds

  • Pike push-up, feet-elevated pike push-up, or wall handstand-push-up progression — 5–10 repetitions.
  • Ring row, cable row, one-arm kettlebell/dumbbell row, or heavy band row — 8–12 repetitions.
  • Rest 45–75 seconds after both exercises.

Strength superset 2 — 2 rounds

  • Goblet squat with kettlebell/dumbbell or front-loaded backpack squat — 8–12 repetitions.
  • Weighted glute bridge, floor hip thrust, or hamstring walkout — 8–15 repetitions.
  • Rest 45–75 seconds after both exercises.

Finisher and optional replacement

  • One or two rounds as time allows: Pallof press from a rated anchor or dead bug with full exhale — 5–8 per side; short-lever Copenhagen plank — 15–25 seconds per side.
  • In an eligible one-badminton week only, optional six-minute HIIT replaces—not follows—the finisher.
  • Use 30 seconds hard / 30 seconds easy with a back-comfortable mode; omit when sleep, back, knees, or badminton recovery is suboptimal.

Progression

Eligible one-badminton week: optional 6-minute HIIT replaces—not follows—the finisher.

Card safety rule

If Tuesday + Wednesday include badminton, move B to Friday and omit C. Never place lower-body strength immediately before badminton. Stop if symptoms worsen, radiate, or technique deteriorates. Follow physio guidance and read the full safety notes.

Safety notes

Stop and arrange clinical assessment for new, changing, recurrent, or radiating symptoms before progressing loaded hinges, unilateral leg work, or plyometrics. Exercises should not cause symptoms that remain meaningfully worse the following day. Skill work, jumps, and loaded strength all stop before technique deteriorates. Seek emergency assessment for saddle numbness or bowel/bladder change. Seek urgent clinical assessment for new or progressive leg weakness.

Readiness rules

  • Green: normal muscle effort or mild stiffness that improves during warm-up—continue.
  • Yellow: localized discomfort, reduced range, or symptoms worsening set to set—reduce range or load, choose a simpler variation, or perform mobility only.
  • Indoor exercises are not automatically safer. Never use an improvised door, furniture edge, or unstable anchor for high-tension pulling.
