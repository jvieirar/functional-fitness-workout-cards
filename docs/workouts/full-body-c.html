SATURDAY · CONDITIONAL THIRD SESSION

Full-body C

This third formal strength session is Saturday only when there is no weekend badminton. Sunday remains a recovery day before Monday Full-body A.

36–42 MINSATURDAY ONLY WHEN THERE IS NO WEEKEND BADMINTON

Complete instructions

This third formal strength session is Saturday only when there is no weekend badminton. Sunday remains a recovery day before Monday Full-body A.

Preparation and skill

  • Daily mobility shortened to 5 minutes.
  • Front-lever or weakest-skill practice — 7 minutes.
  • No mandatory jumps.

Strength superset 1 — 3 rounds

  • Ring dip progression, parallel-bar dip, close-grip push-up, or weighted floor push-up — 5–10 repetitions.
  • Chin-up/pull-up, ring row, cable pulldown, or heavy band pulldown/row — 5–12 repetitions. Any band pulldown requires a rated anchor.
  • Rest 45–75 seconds after both exercises.

Strength superset 2 — 2 rounds

  • Step-up on a verified stable surface or reverse lunge — 6–10 repetitions per side.
  • Kettlebell Romanian deadlift, kickstand RDL, or hamstring walkout — 8–12 repetitions.
  • Rest 45–75 seconds after both exercises.

Finisher and optional replacement

  • Two rounds: bird dog with three-second reach — 5 per side; bear-plank shoulder tap or elevated shoulder tap — 6–10 per side.
  • In an eligible one-badminton week only, optional six-minute HIIT replaces the trunk finisher.
  • Use 30 seconds hard / 30 seconds easy with a back-comfortable mode; omit when sleep, back, knees, or badminton recovery is suboptimal.

Progression

Eligible one-badminton week: optional 6-minute HIIT replaces the trunk finisher.

Card safety rule

Keep Sunday as recovery before Monday A. Omit C after a two-badminton week or if recovery is suboptimal. Stop if symptoms worsen, radiate, or technique deteriorates. Follow physio guidance and read the full safety notes.

Safety notes

Stop and arrange clinical assessment for new, changing, recurrent, or radiating symptoms before progressing loaded hinges, unilateral leg work, or plyometrics. Exercises should not cause symptoms that remain meaningfully worse the following day. Skill work, jumps, and loaded strength all stop before technique deteriorates. Seek emergency assessment for saddle numbness or bowel/bladder change. Seek urgent clinical assessment for new or progressive leg weakness.

Readiness rules

  • Green: normal muscle effort or mild stiffness that improves during warm-up—continue.
  • Yellow: localized discomfort, reduced range, or symptoms worsening set to set—reduce range or load, choose a simpler variation, or perform mobility only.
  • Indoor exercises are not automatically safer. Never use an improvised door, furniture edge, or unstable anchor for high-tension pulling.
