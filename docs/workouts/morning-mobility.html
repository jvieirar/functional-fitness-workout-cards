DAILY · GENTLE PREPARATION

Daily Morning Mobility

Perform this daily sequence gently as preparation. Keep the listed order and use a small, comfortable range.

8–10 MINCOMFORTABLE RANGE · NOT A FLEXIBILITY TEST

Complete instructions

Perform this daily sequence gently as preparation. Keep the listed order and use a small, comfortable range.

Complete sequence

  • Crocodile or 90/90 breathing — 5 slow breaths.
  • Cat-camel — 6 controlled repetitions in a small, comfortable range.
  • Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch with glute squeeze — 30 seconds per side.
  • Open-book thoracic rotation — 5 repetitions per side.
  • Ankle rocks over toes — 8 repetitions per side.
  • Deep squat hold with supported breathing — 30–45 seconds.
  • Glute bridge — 8 controlled repetitions with a two-second hold.
  • Scapular wall slides or band pull-aparts — 8–12 repetitions.

Back-sensitive morning

  • Replace only the deep squat and glute bridge with comfortable walking plus movements previously approved by the physiotherapist.
  • Retain the other comfortable mobility movements; stop or reduce range if symptoms worsen.

Progression

Consistency beats intensity. This sequence prepares the day; it is not a flexibility test.

Card safety rule

Back-sensitive morning: replace only deep squat + bridge with comfortable walking and physio-approved movements. Stop if symptoms worsen, radiate, or technique deteriorates. Follow physio guidance and read the full safety notes.

Safety notes

Stop and arrange clinical assessment for new, changing, recurrent, or radiating symptoms before progressing loaded hinges, unilateral leg work, or plyometrics. Exercises should not cause symptoms that remain meaningfully worse the following day. Skill work, jumps, and loaded strength all stop before technique deteriorates. Seek emergency assessment for saddle numbness or bowel/bladder change. Seek urgent clinical assessment for new or progressive leg weakness.

Readiness rules

  • Green: normal muscle effort or mild stiffness that improves during warm-up—continue.
  • Yellow: localized discomfort, reduced range, or symptoms worsening set to set—reduce range or load, choose a simpler variation, or perform mobility only.
  • Indoor exercises are not automatically safer. Never use an improvised door, furniture edge, or unstable anchor for high-tension pulling.
